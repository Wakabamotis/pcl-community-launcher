# Privacy Policy — PCL Community Launcher

**Last updated:** 2026-11-07

PCL Community Launcher ("the Launcher", "we", "us") is an open-source,
non-commercial third-party launcher for Minecraft: Java Edition. This policy
explains what information the Launcher processes and how it is handled.

**Data controller:** the PCL Community Launcher project maintainers.
**Contact:** <CONTACT_EMAIL>

## 1. Summary

- We do not operate an account server.
- We never ask for your Microsoft password.
- Microsoft authentication tokens are stored locally on your device.
- We do not sell or rent personal information.
- The Launcher communicates directly with Microsoft, Xbox Live, and Minecraft
  Services to authenticate you and verify game ownership.

## 2. Information we process

### 2.1 Microsoft account authentication data

When you sign in with a Microsoft account, the Launcher uses Microsoft's OAuth
2.0 device code flow and receives:

- an OAuth access token and refresh token;
- an Xbox Live token and XSTS token;
- a Minecraft access token;
- your Minecraft UUID and username;
- your Minecraft ownership/entitlement status.

These tokens are stored locally on your device (for example, in the launcher's
configuration or credential store) so that you do not need to sign in every
time. They are sent only to Microsoft, Xbox Live, and Minecraft Services as
part of the authentication flow.

### 2.2 Launcher settings and local data

The Launcher stores settings, logs, downloaded files, game instances, and
diagnostic information locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to us.

### 2.3 Crash reports and diagnostics

If you choose to submit a crash report or diagnostic log, the report may
include:

- launcher and game logs;
- system information (OS version, Java version, memory, GPU);
- the list of installed mods and game versions;
- file paths and error messages.

You can review the report before submitting it. Do not submit reports that
contain information you do not want to share.

### 2.4 Optional telemetry

Some builds may include optional anonymous telemetry, such as launch success
or failure events. The community build does not send telemetry by default. If
telemetry is available, you can disable it in the Launcher's settings.

### 2.5 Website and repository

If you visit our GitHub repository or project website, GitHub or the hosting
provider may process standard technical information such as your IP address,
browser type, and access time according to their own privacy policies.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information only to:

- authenticate you with Microsoft and Xbox Live;
- verify that your account owns Minecraft: Java Edition;
- launch the game and manage game instances;
- diagnose crashes and improve the Launcher;
- respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale.

## 4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we process authentication data because it is
necessary to provide the service you requested. We process diagnostic data
based on your consent when you choose to submit a report or enable telemetry.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share information only:

- with Microsoft, Xbox Live, and Minecraft Services as necessary for
  authentication and ownership verification;
- with GitHub or our hosting provider when you visit the project website;
- with download mirrors such as BMCLAPI, which may receive standard request
  information such as your IP address and the requested file path;
- when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

## 6. International transfers

Microsoft, Xbox Live, and Minecraft Services may process data in countries
other than your own under their own privacy policies. See the Microsoft
Privacy Statement for details.

## 7. Retention

Authentication tokens are retained locally until you sign out, delete the
Launcher's data, or revoke the application's access in your Microsoft account.
Logs and crash reports are retained locally according to your settings, or on
the project's issue tracker if you choose to submit them.

## 8. Security

We use HTTPS for network requests and follow Microsoft's OAuth 2.0 best
practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Protect
your device and Microsoft account with a strong password and multi-factor
authentication.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct,
delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. You can:

- sign out of the Launcher and delete its local data;
- revoke the Launcher's access in your Microsoft account permissions page;
- contact us at <CONTACT_EMAIL> for privacy requests.

## 10. Children's privacy

The Launcher is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date will be
revised, and material changes will be announced in the repository.
